# The motorist's manual: Every Street design language

Every Street is set like a 1960s motorist's manual: warm paper
stock, one body ink, three spot inks, hairline rules, and pen-and-ink plates.
It prints in two editions. The night edition (dark, the default) is cream ink
on dark stock; the day edition (`html.light-mode`) is dark ink on warm paper.

This document is the implementation reference. Every token lives in
`static/css/core/variables.css`, and the guardrails in
`tests/guardrails/test_design_tokens.py` enforce the rules marked below. The
earlier audit and migration record is in
[`atlas-design-system-plan.md`](atlas-design-system-plan.md).

## Governing rule: color is authored, not personalized

The product has one closed palette. There is no user-selectable accent, no
runtime rewriting of tokens, and no `--accent` alias.

- Page CSS contains no raw colors; it uses tokens. *(guardrail)*
- No green hues anywhere in the application palette. *(guardrail)*
- New hues are a design-language change, not a page-local exception.

## Stock and inks

| Token | Printed role |
|---|---|
| `--manual-paper`, `--manual-paper-shade` | Page stock, and the shaded stock behind panels and hovers |
| `--manual-ink`, `--manual-ink-soft`, `--manual-ink-faint` | Body ink and two lighter impressions |
| `--manual-solid` | The heaviest fill: solid blocks and the car's body |
| `--manual-rule`, `--manual-rule-faint` | Hairline rules |
| `--manual-navy` | Primary spot ink: driven streets, progress, links, live data |
| `--manual-rust`, `--manual-rust-ink` | Danger, undriven streets, the focus ring; `-ink` is the text-weight tint |
| `--manual-mustard` | Planned routes, attention, work in progress |
| `--manual-grain`, `--manual-grid` | Paper grain and the squared survey grid behind small drawings |

The semantic tokens (`--primary`, `--success`, `--warning`, `--danger`,
`--info`, `--surface-*`, `--text-*`, `--border-*`) are mixed from these inks.
Use a semantic token when the meaning is semantic (an error, a disabled
control) and a `--manual-*` token when the look is printed (a rule, a plate,
a stamped label). Buttons use `--action`, `--action-hover`, and
`--text-on-action`: ink on paper by day, paper on ink by night.

## Printing rules

- **Corners are square or nearly so.** Use the `--radius-*` tokens (1 to
  4px). `--radius-full` is for dots, knobs, and other true circles only.
- **Shadows are flat offsets, never blurred** (`--shadow-*`), and panels are
  opaque. Structure comes from 1px rules and type, not floating layers.
- **Focus is a rust ring**: `--focus-ring`, `--focus-ring-strong`, or
  `--focus-outline`.
- **Cards are for discrete objects** such as a vehicle, place, or coverage
  area. Everything else sits on the page between rules.

## Typography

Three faces carry the whole app:

| Role | Token | Face |
|---|---|---|
| Everything you read or operate, and measured numbers | `--font-family-text` (also `--font-family`, `--font-numeric`) | Source Serif 4 |
| Titles and headline figures | `--font-family-display` | Roboto Serif, one condensed heavy cut (opsz 144, wdth 72, wght 800) |
| Short uppercase labels: eyebrows, buttons, table heads, running heads | `--font-family-label` | Libre Franklin |
| Machine output | `--font-family-mono` | Platform monospace |

- **The display cut is drawn for large sizes.** Page titles and headline
  figures use it; h3 to h6 and small titles use the text face in bold.
- **Monospace means machine, not measurement.** Logs, raw payloads, URLs, and
  opaque identifiers get the mono face. Every number the app measured stays
  in the text face with tabular lining figures.
- **Size comes from the scale** (`--font-size-*`), never a literal. 12px is
  the floor. *(guardrail; `em` sizes are allowed)*
- **Tracking comes from the tracking tokens.** Body text is untracked;
  condensed display type takes `--tracking-display`; printed uppercase labels
  take `--tracking-label` or `--tracking-caps`. *(guardrail)*
- **Uppercase is for short labels only.** Navigation, tabs, headings,
  sentences, and values stay in sentence case.
- **Prose is measured**: running text is capped at `--measure`, a masthead
  subtitle at `--measure-tight`.
- Never set `font-size` on `html` from a rem token; it rescales every size and
  spacing token. *(guardrail)*

## Plates

The pen-and-ink illustrations are inline SVG partials in
`templates/partials/plates/`, generated by
`scripts/generate_landing_plates.py`. Their shared SVG definitions
(`_plate_defs.html`) load once from `base.html`, and their styles
(`.plate-ink`, `.pl-*`) live in `components/manual.css`. Plates draw in
`currentColor`, so they print in both editions without separate artwork.

- A standard page may print one plate beside its masthead with
  `.pm-plate` (`--wide` for landscapes, `--car` for the Murano). Plates are
  `aria-hidden` and hide on phones.
- The Murano engraving (`.car-engraving`) masks the
  `static/images/landing/murano-*.webp` layers: ink lines, solid body, and rust
  lamps. Its license plate stays visible.

## Component canon

| Part | Canonical API | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Masthead | `.page-masthead` with `.pm-lead` (`.pm-eyebrow`, `.pm-title`, `.pm-sub`), optional `.pm-aside` and `.pm-plate` | One introduction per standard page. *(guardrail: templates need a masthead unless exempted)* |
| Measured data | `.figure-band`, `.figure-label`, `.figure-value`, `.data-num` | Totals and measured values |
| Object card | `.card.card--object` | A truly discrete object |
| Tabs | `.atlas-tabs`, `.atlas-tab` | Panel switching with an ink underline |
| Segmented control | `.segmented-control` | Compact view or mode switching |
| Stepper | `.atlas-stepper`, `.atlas-step`, `.atlas-step-index`, `.atlas-step-label` | Multi-step workflows |
| Buttons | `.btn-primary`, `.btn-secondary`, `.btn-ghost`, `.btn-danger` | Ink primary, ruled secondary, quiet ghost, destructive danger |
| Forms | `components/forms.css` | Paper fill, ruled border, rust focus ring |
| Progress | `.survey-bar`, `.survey-fill` | A ruled bar filled in navy; `.is-working` while a job runs |
| Empty state | `.empty-state` family | Dashed rule, one line icon, one sentence, at most one quiet action |

Page CSS may compose these and add layout, but must not recreate their
appearance.

## Stylesheet organization

- `templates/partials/_assets.html` is the stylesheet manifest.
  `global_stylesheets` load on every page in cascade order;
  `map_feature_stylesheets` and `live_navigation_stylesheets` are shared
  bundles; each page adds `static/css/pages/<page>.css` in its `extra_css`
  block. Files are served from `/static-v/<version>/` with immutable caching.
- Pages load `mapbox-gl.css` after the global stylesheets. Global rules that
  override Mapbox classes are scoped under `.mapboxgl-map` so they outrank the
  Mapbox rule they replace (see `components/mapbox-overrides.css`).

## Map and chart colors

| Meaning | Token |
|---|---|
| Driven streets, saved places | `--map-driven`, `--map-place`, `--map-place-outline` |
| Undriven streets | `--map-undriven` |
| Undriveable streets | `--map-undriveable` |
| An individual trip | `--map-trip-path`; selected: `--map-trip-path-selected` |
| Planned route, selected street | `--map-route` |
| Halo under lines and pins | `--basemap-halo` |

- JavaScript reads tokens with `readToken()` from
  `static/js/modules/core/theme-tokens.js` (`readMapColor()` for map paint).
  Feature modules do not hardcode hex colors or keep hex fallbacks. Map page
  layers name a `colorToken` in `CONFIG.LAYER_DEFAULTS`, resolved by
  `layerColor()` in `map-styles.js` when the layer is painted; legends use
  `var()` directly. Layers that must follow a theme toggle repaint on the
  `themeChanged` event. Tests that need token values use
  `tests/helpers/theme-tokens.js`.
- Deliberate literals: the share film's palette, the trip and matched-trip
  heat ramps, the Google Maps style JSON, invisible hit-area lines, and the
  theme-color meta constants.
- Mapbox GL accepts hex and comma-separated `rgb()` only; convert modern
  color syntax before passing it to a paint property.
- `static/js/modules/maps/manual-basemap.js` reprints the Mapbox light and
  dark styles in the `--basemap-*` inks and reprints them on theme change.
  Satellite and street styles are left as published.
- Chart.js takes its fonts, inks, tooltip, and square bars from the global
  theme in `core/library-loader.js`, re-applied on `themeChanged`. Page charts
  set layout and series colors only. Categorical series use at most six
  colors in this fixed order: `--cat-cobalt`, `--cat-ochre`, `--cat-steel`,
  `--cat-coral`, `--cat-slate`, `--cat-purple`.

### Coverage share film

The City of Light share film is a fixed piece of export artwork. Its charcoal
blue background, warm paper typography, and illuminated brass streets stay the
same in both application themes. These canvas colors are authored in the share
renderer; they do not redefine the interactive map inks. The surrounding share
dialog uses the standard tokens and controls. The film and its PNG closing
frame use Source Serif 4 and Libre Franklin, and retain OpenStreetMap
attribution.

The reveal follows recorded first-drive timestamps. Streets without a recorded
date are visible from the beginning. Mileage and percentages are calculated
from the same complete geometry snapshot; undriveable streets are excluded
from the denominator. Exports contain no vehicle identifiers or live-trip data.

## Copy

Write like the manual: plain, specific, and short. Say what a page holds or
what a control does ("Stops at your saved places, over time."). No mission
statements, no slogans, and no cheerful filler. Name a page for what it
holds; the app is not described as an atlas or a personal record.

## Responsive and accessible behavior

- Build mobile-first; add complexity as space becomes available.
- Interactive controls have a 44px touch target (`--touch-target`) and a
  visible token-based focus treatment.
- Never require hover to reveal essential information or actions.
- Preserve reading and keyboard order when layouts reflow.
- Check both editions at 375, 768, 1024, and 1440 CSS pixels.
- Respect reduced motion and safe-area insets for fixed mobile controls.

## Change checklist

1. Reuse a canonical component and token; do not fork a page-local look.
2. Keep the palette closed: no raw colors in page CSS and no green hues.
3. Keep measured numbers in the text face with tabular figures, and keep
   monospace for machine output.
4. Check both editions and the four reference widths.
5. Run the design guardrails (`tests/guardrails/test_design_tokens.py`) and
   the JavaScript tests.
